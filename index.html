// profile

I'm an Energy Data Scientist with over 8 years of experience at the intersection of energy systems and data engineering. Based in Milan, I work on revenue forecasting, portfolio analytics and machine learning for the renewable energy sector — currently as Senior Energy Markets Analyst at Nadara.

My background combines a MSc in Energy Engineering from Politecnico di Milano (110/110 cum laude) with hands-on experience in Switzerland at Romande Énergie, where I built ML models for time series forecasting and anomaly detection, and helped bring a community solar product to market in collaboration with the Swiss Data Science Center at EPFL.

I've published peer-reviewed research in Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews and Applied Energy, and since 2022 I teach two modules on energy digitalisation and time series analysis in the Master in IT Management at HES-SO Lausanne.
