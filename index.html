// profile

I'm an Energy Data Scientist with 9 years of experience at the intersection of energy systems and data engineering. Based in Milan, I work on revenue forecasting, portfolio analytics and machine learning for the renewable energy sector — currently as Senior Energy Markets Analyst at Nadara, one of the biggest independent wind energy producers in Europe

My background combines a MSc in Energy Engineering from Politecnico di Milano (110/110 cum laude) with hands-on experience in Switzerland at Romande Énergie, where I built ML models for time series forecasting and anomaly detection n collaboration with the Swiss Data Science Center at EPFL, and helped bring a community solar product to market. 5 years in the analytics team built strong foundations on agile development, data visualisation and translating business needs into technical solutions.

I've published peer-reviewed research in Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews and Applied Energy, and since 2022 I teach two modules on energy digitalisation and time series analysis in the Master in IT Management at HES-SO Lausanne.

I care about how teams are built as much as what they build. I've spent a good part of my career being the bridge between technical and business people — translating models into decisions, and decisions back into models. I tend to think that sustainable software, like sustainable energy, is mostly about the long game: doing things right rather than fast, and investing in people before processes. Outside of work I play bass and guitar and try to convince notes and words to become songs.
